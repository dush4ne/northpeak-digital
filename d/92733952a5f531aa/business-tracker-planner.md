# Freelance Business Tracker & Weekly Planner
*by Northpeak Digital*

A simple system to see where your money and time go. Copy the tables into a spreadsheet (Google Sheets, Excel, Notion) or keep this file as your notebook.

---

## 1. Income log

| Date | Client | Project | Invoice # | Amount | Due | Paid? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | |

**Monthly total** = sum of Amount where Paid = yes.
**Outstanding** = sum of Amount where Paid = no. Check it every Friday.

## 2. Expense log

| Date | Vendor | Category | Amount | Receipt saved? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |

Suggested categories: software, equipment, education, marketing, insurance, professional fees, travel, other. Keep receipts. Ask your accountant what's deductible where you live.

| Billable hours | | |

**Your effective hourly rate is the number most freelancers never check.** If it is much lower than your target rate, you're underpricing, overservicing, or losing hours to unpaid work.

## 6. Friday review (10 minutes)

- What did I finish? [ ]
- What took longer than I estimated, and by how much? [ ]

## 7. Quarterly check (30 minutes)

- Income by client: does one client make up more than 40%? That's a risk. Plan to diversify.
- Raise rates? If you're booked more than 80% for two months in a row, yes.
- Fire a client? Any client who pays late, scope-creeps, and is below your average rate is costing you money.
- Tax: have you set aside your percentage? Do you owe estimated payments? (Rules vary; confirm with a professional.)
- Goals for the next quarter: [income / clients / skill / time off]
