# Freelancer Client Onboarding Kit
*by Northpeak Digital*

Copy, paste, and adapt. Replace [BRACKETS] with your details.

---

## 1. Welcome email (send right after a client says yes)

**Subject:** Welcome aboard, [CLIENT NAME]. Here's what happens next

Hi [CLIENT NAME],

Thanks for choosing me for [PROJECT]. I'm excited to get started.

Here's how the next two weeks will go:
1. **Today:** You'll get the project questionnaire (below) and an invoice for the [50]% deposit.
2. **Within 2 business days of the deposit:** I'll send a kickoff plan with milestones and dates.
3. **Weekly:** A short Friday update on progress and what I need from you.

To keep things on schedule, I need two things from you by [DATE]: the completed questionnaire and any logins or files listed in it.

Questions at any point? Reply here.

[YOUR NAME]

## 2. Project questionnaire (send as a form or doc)

1. In one sentence, what does success look like for this project?
2. Who is the audience or customer?
3. Three examples of work you like, and one thing you dislike about each.
4. Hard deadline? What drives it (launch, event, season)?
5. Who gives final approval? Is anyone else involved?
6. What do you already have (brand assets, copy, logins, data)?
7. What should I *not* touch or change?
8. Budget range, if the scope is flexible.
9. Preferred communication: email, call, chat? Best hours and time zone?
10. Anything that worried you on past projects like this?

---

## 3. Scope and agreement checklist

Before work starts, your written agreement should state:
- [ ] Exact deliverables, in a bulleted list
- [ ] What is *out* of scope
- [ ] Number of revision rounds included (e.g., 2) and the price of extra rounds
- [ ] Timeline with milestone dates
- [ ] Total price, deposit amount, and when each payment is due
- [ ] Late-payment terms (e.g., [1.5]% per month after [14] days)
- [ ] Who owns the work, and when ownership transfers (usually on full payment)
- [ ] How either side can end the project
- [ ] Contact for approvals and how approval is confirmed (reply "approved" by email)

*This kit is a set of templates, not legal advice. Have a professional review your contract for your situation.*

## 6. Invoice follow-ups

**Day 1 after due date**
Subject: Friendly reminder: invoice [#] due
Hi [NAME], just a quick reminder that invoice [#] for [AMOUNT] was due on [DATE]. The payment link is here: [LINK]. If it's already on its way, thank you!

**Day 7 after due date**
Subject: Invoice [#] is now 7 days overdue
Hi [NAME], invoice [#] for [AMOUNT] is now a week past due. Could you let me know when I can expect payment? If something is blocking it, tell me and we'll sort it out.

**Day 14 after due date**
Subject: Action needed: invoice [#]
Hi [NAME], invoice [#] is 14 days overdue. Per our agreement, late fees of [X]% now apply and I'll need to pause work until it's settled. Please pay here: [LINK], or reply today if there's an issue.
